How delivery works

  1. You place the order. Give us a name, a Ghanaian phone number we can reach you on, your region, your town and a nearby landmark.
  2. We confirm it with you. Our team calls the number on the order to confirm the exact device and the delivery arrangement.
  3. We pack and dispatch. Confirmed orders leave us within 48 hours.
  4. You inspect and pay. The rider hands over your order, you check it on the spot, then you pay on delivery.

Have this ready

  • Your name as it should appear on the order.
  • A working Ghanaian number —053 231 9277 and +233 53 231 9277 are both accepted.
  • Your region and town, chosen from the checkout list.
  • A landmark or short note that helps the rider find you (optional).

When the rider arrives

  • Check the model, storage and colour against your order.
  • Look over the device and its screen before paying.
  • Pay the order total, then keep your order ID for any warranty questions.

If something goes wrong

  • If the device is not what you ordered, do not pay — hand it back to the rider and tell us the same day.
  • If you miss the rider, we will try your number again and reschedule. After repeated failed attempts the order may be cancelled.
  • Every order and its status is listed on your Orders page.
