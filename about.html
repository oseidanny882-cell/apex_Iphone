Who we are

Apex iPhone is a Ghanaian phone store based in Akropong-Akuapem. We sell iPhones and the accessories our customers actually ask for: chargers, cables, cases, screen protectors and power banks.

We started because buying a used iPhone in Ghana was too often a gamble — unclear condition, no support after the sale, and a price that changed depending on who was asking. Our answer is straightforward: describe every device accurately, price it clearly in GH₵, deliver it to your door, and let you pay only once you have seen it.

What we stand for

  • Honest condition reports — storage, colour, and any marks or battery notes are stated before you buy.
  • Real prices — the total shown when you place your order is the total you pay on delivery.
  • Support after the sale — warranty questions and faults are handled by the same team that sold you the phone.
  • Nationwide reach — we deliver beyond Accra, to every region the checkout accepts.

How an order works

  1. Choose your device. Browse the shop, compare models, and add what you want to your cart.
  2. Give us your delivery details. Your name, a Ghanaian phone number we can reach you on, your region, your town, and a nearby landmark.
  3. We confirm the order. Someone from our team calls the number on the order to confirm the exact device and the delivery arrangement.
  4. Delivery and payment. The rider brings your order, you check it on the spot, then you pay on delivery.
