How do I place an order? ▸

Add what you want to your cart, open the cart and continue to checkout. You will be asked for your name, a Ghanaian phone number, your region, your town, and a nearby landmark so the rider can find you.Only if you are Akropong-Akuapem

Do I need an account to buy? ▸

Yes. Your cart and wishlist are kept against your account on our servers, so they are still there the next time you sign in — even from a different phone or laptop.

Which payment methods do you accept? ▸

Pay on delivery. You pay the rider once you have checked your device. Nothing is charged when you place the order, and this site never asks for card or mobile-money details.

Is delivery free? ▸

Delivery is free on orders above GH₵2,500.00 within Akropong-Akuapem.

How long will my order take? ▸

We dispatch within 48 hours of confirming your order.

Do you deliver to my region? ▸

No. We don't do delivery but if you are in Akropong-Akuapem we will send the product to your door step.

Can I order accessories together with a phone? ▸

No. Cases, chargers, cables, screen protectors and power banks can go into the same cart as your iPhone and arrive in the same delivery

How do you describe the condition of a phone? ▸

Each listing states the model, storage, colour and condition, and pre-owned units note the marks and battery health we measured. If you want more detail before paying, ask us and we will send extra photos or the serial information for that device.

Is there a warranty? ▸

New devices carry the manufacturer's warranty, and pre-owned devices carry our store warranty. Ask us for the cover that applies to the exact device you are buying and we will confirm it before dispatch.

Can I change or cancel my order? ▸

Yes, at any time before the order is dispatched — send us the order ID shown on your Orders page. If you want to change the model, storage or delivery details, tell us during the confirmation call or message us before dispatch.

What if the device develops a fault? ▸

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and a short description; photos or a video help us move faster. A fault covered by warranty is repaired or replaced. Accidental damage, liquid damage and unauthorised repairs are not covered.

How do I track my order? ▸

Your Orders page lists every order with its status: pending while we confirm the details, confirmed once we have spoken to you, delivered after you receive it, or cancelled if the order was called off.

I forgot my password — what now? ▸

On the login page choose Forgot password and we will email you a 6-digit code. The code is valid for 15 minutes. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder and request a new one.

Still need help?

Send us the order ID from your Orders page and a short description of what happened, and we will take it from there.
