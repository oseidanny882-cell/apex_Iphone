Privacy policy

Ghana iPhone Store · Prices in GHS · Secure server-side checkout.

Last updated: 25 September 2026 Applies to this website and your account Requests: Contact us

1. Who we are

Apex iPhone is a phone store based in Akropong-Akuapem, Ghana. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers this store only — not other websites you reach from links here.

2. Information you give us

  • Account details — your name, email address and phone number, plus your password. Passwords are stored only as a salted Argon2id hash, so nobody at Apex iPhone can read your password.
  • Order and delivery details — the name, phone number, region, town, landmark and rider note you give at checkout, and the items you ordered.
  • Messages you send us — whatever you tell us by email or phone about an order, a warranty claim or your account.

3. Information we collect automatically

  • Server logs — the technical data needed to keep the site running and safe, such as your IP address and the times of your requests. We use it for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Your cart and wishlist — stored against your account on our servers rather than in a tracking cookie in your browser.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect card or mobile-money details, because orders are paid on delivery. We do not run third-party advertising, analytics or social tracking scripts on this site, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

5. How we use your information

  • To confirm and deliver your order, including calling the number you gave us.
  • To run your account: your cart, wishlist, order history and password resets.
  • To support you after the sale, including warranty claims and returns.
  • To protect the store: preventing fraudulent orders, abuse and unauthorised access.
  • To keep records we are required to keep for accounting and tax.

6. Cookies and browser storage

When you sign in we set one session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and it is removed when you sign out. The password-reset flow briefly keeps the email address and code you are using in your browser's session storage; that is cleared when you finish or close the tab. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Who we share it with

  • Delivery riders and couriers receive the name, phone number and address details needed to bring you the order.
  • Our email provider sends the password-reset codes we email to you.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and we do not pass it to advertisers.

8. How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id and are never stored or emailed in plain text.
  • Sessions use a secure, HttpOnly cookie, and password-reset codes are short-lived and stored hashed.
  • Sign-in, order and password-reset endpoints are rate limited against automated guessing.
  • Account and admin changes are written to an audit log, so unusual activity can be traced.

9. How long we keep it

We keep account and order records while your account is active, and afterwards for as long as we need them for warranty, accounting and dispute purposes. You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal information; we will do so, except for records.

10. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to a particular use. Some details you can change yourself on your account page; for anything else, contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Age & Parental Guidance Notice

While our platform is open to responsible younger users, anyone under 18 must have permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian to use this app. We do not knowingly collect personal details from unsupervised minors. If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account without your consent, please contact us to have it removed.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. Please check back whenever you want to know the current position.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us. See also our terms of service and FAQ.
